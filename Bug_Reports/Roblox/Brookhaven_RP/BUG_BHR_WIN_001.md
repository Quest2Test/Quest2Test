## Description

### Steps to Reproduce
**Prerequisites:** Valid Roblox account

1. Load into Brookhaven RP game.
2. Walk towards doors inside buildings at the following locations:
  a. Inside Jetts, Janitor Door.
  b. Inside Jetts, Toilet Door.
  c. Inside Jetts, Managers Door.
  d. Inside Airplane, Toilet Door.
  e. Inside Hospital, All Doors.
  f. Inside Brookhaven High, Stage Helpers Door.
3. Monitor the character as it walks through the door without the door moving.

### Expected Behaviour
As the character encounters the door, the door should swing open to allow the character to walk through the doorway.

### Actual Behaviour
The door does not move; the character walks directly through the solid door.
